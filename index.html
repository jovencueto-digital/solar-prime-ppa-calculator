How this estimate works
  • Bill today = annual kWh × utility rate ÷ 12. The utility rate is an all-in average (supply, delivery and fixed charges).
  • Solar PPA payment = annual kWh × share covered by solar × PPA rate ÷ 12.
  • Remaining utility bill = the fixed monthly charge plus the kWh solar doesn't cover, at the utility's per-kWh rate.
  • Long-term savings assume the utility price and PPA rate rise by the yearly percentages above.

Estimates only, for average calculation purposes. Actual savings depend on the home's roof, system size, solar production, utility tariff, net metering rules and the final PPA terms. Default rates are 2026 statewide averages and can be changed under “Adjust rates and assumptions.” This is not a quote or an offer.
